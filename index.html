Hermes Agent

A private, single-user automation agent.

Hermes Agent is a personal automation tool operated by one individual for their own Google Account. It runs locally on the operator's own computer and connects to Google APIs with OAuth 2.0 using credentials the operator created and controls. There is no public sign-up, no other users, and no server-side service offered to third parties.

What it does

  • Reads the operator's own Gmail to find messages from the operator's child's school and extract calendar details such as minimum-day and early-dismissal notices.
  • Sends those reminders to the operator through the operator's own messaging app.
  • May read the operator's own Google Calendar to cross-check scheduling.

Google data it requests

  • gmail.readonly — read the operator's own mailbox. Messages cannot be modified, sent, or deleted.
  • calendar — read the operator's own calendars.

No other Google data is requested. The agent has no access to any Google Account other than the operator's own.

Data handling

Data retrieved from Google stays on the operator's own machine. It is not transmitted to, stored on, or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
